Method

Measure first. Teach second. Do not invent a title to carry the sentence.

Rules that stay on the page

  • Knowledge is free. No visit required. Online only.
  • White Hat only. Commands are metaphors on your own machines. Nothing is executed against a third party.
  • Consent first. Attribution and reciprocity. Small shared core, local project.
  • School record and platform exams stay on two lines. They are not the same object. Platform exams live on Exams.
  • Affiliate work is marked in the sentence when money is involved.
